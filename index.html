Design your relationships your way

About This Tool

The Relationship Anarchy Smorgasbord is a tool for exploring and communicating about the different elements that can be part of a relationship. It's based on the idea that relationships can be customized to fit the needs and desires of the people involved, rather than following preset templates.

Select the elements that interest you, and use different colors to indicate your level of interest or comfort. You can share your results with partners to facilitate conversations about what you each want from your relationship.
